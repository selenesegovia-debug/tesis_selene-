## 2. Calidad postcosecha y pérdida de agua

[doi:10.1016/j.postharvbio.2023.112643](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.postharvbio.2023.112643) · Firmeza inicial y tamaño de baya predicen el comportamiento posterior; fórmula de pérdida de peso usada aquí.
- **Oh *et al.* (2024b).** Prediction of blueberry sensory texture attributes by integrating multiple instrumental measurements. *Postharvest Biol. Technol.* 218:113160. [doi:10.1016/j.postharvbio.2024.113160](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.postharvbio.2024.113160)

- **Rivera *et al.* (2021).** Influence of water loss on mechanical properties of stored blueberries. *Postharvest Biol. Technol.* 176:111498. [doi:10.1016/j.postharvbio.2021.111498](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.postharvbio.2021.111498)

- **Yan *et al.* (2020).** Changes of the aroma composition and other quality traits of blueberry ‘Garden Blue’ during cold storage and shelf life. *Foods* 9(9):1223. [doi:10.3390/foods9091223](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods9091223)
